# Product

Treat product geometry, proportions, material, color, logo, label, typography, cap, closure, and packaging
as invariants unless explicitly changed.

- Metal: reflection strips, smooth reflection gradients, negative flags.
- Glass: edge light, transmission/backlight control, clean caustic restraint.
- Cosmetic bottle: vertical reflection strips, label preservation, controlled cap highlight.
- Food: grazing light, moisture highlights, texture definition without artificial saturation.
- Frosted acrylic: broad diffused source and transmission gradient.

Replace “premium/luxury/high-end” with intentional hierarchy, negative space, material response, surface
microdetail, controlled contrast, and brand-appropriate palette.
