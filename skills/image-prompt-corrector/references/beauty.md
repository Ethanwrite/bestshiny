# Beauty and fashion

## Makeup design

Specify only requested makeup variables: foundation/skin finish, blush placement, eyeliner geometry,
lash density, eyeshadow diffusion, brow grooming, lip finish, edge definition, and specularity.

## Beauty photography

Control T-zone and cheekbone highlights, catchlight shape, under-eye fill, lip reflections, hair
separation, facial sculpting, and texture retention. Prefer authentic fine texture, subtle visible pores,
controlled subsurface scattering, and natural microtexture. Never use “flawless plastic skin.”
