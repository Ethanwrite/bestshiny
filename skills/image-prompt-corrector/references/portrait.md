# Portrait

Preserve facial structure, proportions, skin tone, hairline, recognizable asymmetry, wardrobe, pose,
expression, and camera unless explicitly editable.

Enhance through subject-background separation, coherent eye-light, readable facial planes, natural skin
microtexture, controlled highlights, and a composition matching the intended relationship. Avoid
automatic glamour retouching, face reshaping, plastic skin, or an 85mm/shallow-depth default.
