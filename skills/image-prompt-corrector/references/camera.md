# Camera and composition decisions

Choose camera language from the visual objective, never from a fixed task-to-lens lookup.

- Shot size: extreme close-up, close-up, medium, three-quarter, full body, wide, establishing, macro.
- Angle/height: eye level, profile, three-quarter, overhead, high/low angle; state why the relationship
  changes.
- Perspective: 20–28mm equivalents exaggerate spatial depth; 35–50mm retains environment; 85–105mm
  can preserve facial proportions in a tight beauty view. These are options, not defaults.
- Distance and depth: describe subject-background relationship and depth separation before choosing
  shallow or deep focus.
- Composition: centered symmetry, thirds, negative space, leading lines, editorial crop, or macro detail
  must support the product or subject hierarchy.

Do not add a camera or focal length when the user requested only a targeted edit that must preserve the
original camera, framing, and perspective.
