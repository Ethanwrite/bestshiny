Create with BestShiny Director

Tell the director what you want to make

One rough sentence is enough. The director asks what's missing, then walks you through brief → key visuals → visual bible → beats → shots.

Creative brief

Proposed

Approval freezes the brief and hands it to the director, who writes the treatment and screenplay next. Nothing is generated until you approve the screenplay.

Treatment & screenplay

Drafting

The structured screenplay, exactly as the director writes it: one primary action or one line per shot. Saving creates a new revision; only the revision you approve is compiled.

Approving derives the key visuals from the brief and this exact screenplay revision, then generates them through the ordinary image pipeline — each one priced and reserved like any generation.

Key visuals

Waiting

Required visuals (characters, style plate, a commerce product) must be ready before the bible can be drafted. An optional one that failed can be retried or skipped, on record.

Visual bible

Draft

Locking creates each character's canonical identity version and the project's style lock through the platform's own services. If a lock fails, compilation is blocked until it succeeds.

Story beats & shot plan

Proposed

Edit a line, a staging note or a shot's duration here; approval saves your edits as the screenplay revision that is built into the same scenes, shots and continuity graph a written script produces — then you continue in Director.
