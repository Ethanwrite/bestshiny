# Commercial image

Lock product invariants and exact claim/offer text first. Define the communication objective, audience,
hero hierarchy, usage context, negative space for copy, brand palette, material response, and compliance
constraints. Separate marketplace main image, detail/infographic, lifestyle, campaign hero, and social ad
intent instead of combining every asset type in one frame.

Do not invent benefits, ingredients, awards, logos, labels, or offer text. Do not let atmosphere obscure
the product silhouette or mandatory packaging details.
