# Lighting

Make lighting a physical plan rather than a list of fixture names.

## Specify

1. State motivation: window, sky, practical, sun, signage, or studio source justified by the scene.
2. State key direction, height, size, distance, hardness, intensity, and falloff.
3. State fill direction and key-to-fill relationship; use negative fill when contour needs control.
4. State color temperature relationships and exposure priority.
5. State shadow density, rim control, background separation, and specular control.
6. Match the plan to material response: reflection gradients for metal, edge/transmission light for glass, broad
   diffusion for frosted surfaces, and grazing light for surface texture.
7. For composites or relighting, evaluate foreground and background illumination separately, then harmonize
   direction, intensity, color, contact shadow, and spill.

## Preserve

- Preserve established source direction, time of day, practical locations, and exposure across connected shots.
- Preserve authentic skin microtexture; control T-zone and lip highlights without producing plastic skin.
- Preserve labels, logos, cap edges, glass transmission, and product color.
- Support the approved emotion without changing identity, pose, environment, or story facts.

Return `motivation`, `key`, `fill`, `negative_fill`, `rim`, `background`, `temperature`, `exposure_priority`,
`material_controls`, and `continuity_checks`.
