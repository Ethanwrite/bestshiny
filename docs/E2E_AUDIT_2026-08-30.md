## 3. Product findings (High unless noted)

- **H1 — The director cannot take corrections.** Merge never overwrites: the user's
  explicit "地点就是城市天台" and a full look description changed nothing (revision 2
  kept both errors). List-valued fields (`characters`) are entirely opaque to the merge
  — a look can never be added, a second character can never join by conversation.
- **H2 — Bad extraction poisons everything downstream.** `setting.location` swallowed a
  whole clause; it became the scene anchor prompt, the scene heading, and the action
  line "雨桐进入城市天台上发现了一部不属于她的手机". The character's look was dropped, so
  the character anchor renders without the defining wardrobe.
- **H3 — Turn failures burn state.** The user turn commits before the model call: a 500
  leaves an orphaned session (one exists on the account), consumes a FREE round, and
  the failed turn's content (the user's cliffhanger!) is visible in the transcript but
  never merged — trust-damaging.
- **H4 — The director flow never locks identity or style.** Character anchors don't
  become `CharacterIdentityVersion`s; the style plate doesn't lock project style. Face,
  wardrobe, hair and style drift across compiled shots are therefore unguarded
  (Character Evidence dispatch skips `NO_CONFIRMED_IDENTITY_REFERENCES`; the frame
  anchor planner downgrades `NO_CANONICAL_CHARACTER_REFERENCE`). Hair/costume are
  additionally `UNAVAILABLE` in the evidence stack by declaration.
- **H5 — FREE 3-image quota vs up to 6 key visuals per brief** (3 characters + scene +
  product + style): a rich brief structurally cannot finish; the minimal brief consumes
  the entire quota.
- **H6 — Failed key visuals are unrecoverable in place**: `visuals/execute` replays the
  same dead job (idempotency), and even never-sent jobs are `safe_to_retry=false`
  (canary refusal → `PERMANENT_ERROR`). Only a new brief revision (new action keys)
  recovers — which costs a conversation round.
- **M — storyboard quality** (medium): beats are template instantiations — canned
  dialogue ("你终于来了") contradicts a solo-character premise; only the protagonist
  reaches shots; 38s produced for a 30s brief; the style plate prompt is the raw idea
  text; key-visual prompts skip the platform's own prompt corrector.
- **M — latency** (medium): 19–25s per director turn — `doubao-seed-2.0-lite` runs a
  reasoning chain (719 reasoning tokens observed) for a JSON-extraction task; disable
  thinking or use a non-reasoning call, and stream or make the turn async in the UI.
- **M — token usage recorded but never priced**: `model_execution_records` holds exact
  token counts with `actual_cost_usd NULL, cost_source UNKNOWN` — this is also what
  starves canary settlement (C5).

## 4. Recommended fixes (priority order)

1. **Canary economics (C5)** — pass a token-derived estimate from the pricing engine
   into `execute_chat`; settle chat usages from `token_usage × canonical token pricing`
   (`cost_source=TOKENS_LIST`); make EXHAUSTED recoverable when settled holds fall
   below the ceiling; classify canary refusals as retryable. Without this, live chat
   and refine are unusable at any permit size, and every generation needs a
   permit-per-job.
   **Done 2026-08-30.** `TokenCostEngine` (cost_core) prices holds and settlements
   from the dated `model_pricing_profiles` token rows; `ModelRoleRuntime` reserves a
   bounded margin hold instead of the whole budget, settles from counted tokens, and
   records `cost_source=TOKENS_LIST` on the execution record (closing the
   `actual_cost_usd NULL` finding in §3); `settle`/`reconcile_uncertain` hand freed
   budget back (EXHAUSTED→ACTIVE while unexpired and under `max_requests`); a gateway
   canary refusal now schedules `RETRY_WAIT` (RATE_LIMIT) exactly like "no ready
   account" — minting a permit un-blocks the same job — while the
   uncertain/settled-resubmission refusal stays terminal. Unpriced models keep the
   old conservative whole-budget hold and stay UNCERTAIN. The ceiling's exposure rule
   is unchanged and stays sound: cost-EXHAUSTED permits already carry
   `actual+held ≈ authorisation`, and request-EXHAUSTED permits never recover.
   Pinned by `tests/test_canary_economics.py`.
2. **`refine_prompt` must catch `LiveCanaryDenied`** per call and degrade to
   `local_safe_fallback` (the director got this fix; refine still 500s).
   **Done 2026-08-30**, both refiner legs; the route also hands the FREE
   deep-optimization unit back when the result degraded to the unoptimized prompt.
3. **Brief merge**: user-turn extractions may overwrite (per-field provenance), lists
   merge by key (characters by name), and a brief-card field-edit UI.
4. **Post-bible identity/style locking step** (one explicit click) so compiled shots
   inherit locked faces and a locked style gate.
5. Beat planner: use the `hook`/cliffhanger fields; respect the duration budget by
   trimming shots, not only whole beats; pass key-visual prompts through the image
   corrector; distill the logline.
6. FREE quota: either 6 images or per-brief anchor budgeting.

## 8. Optimized storyboard prompts (the quality bar)

Premise repaired: 城市天台 (city rooftop); 雨桐 — young photographer, black trench coat,
shoulder-length hair, film camera on a strap; rainy night, neon spill, cold cinematic
palette; solo-character suspense; cliffhanger: the ringing phone shows caller "雨桐".
Shared constraints on every shot: 同一人物同一造型（黑风衣/齐肩短发/胶片相机），同一天台
同一雨夜，冷色电影感；9:16 竖屏。Negative: 无其他人物、无字幕水印、不切换画风。

- **SH01 WIDE 5s（建立，re-anchor）** 雨夜城市天台全景，竖构图。雨桐从天台门走入画面深处，
  背影小而孤立。冷蓝主调，远处霓虹在湿地面反光，雨丝在逆光中可见。高机位缓慢下摇，35mm
  电影质感，浅景深收在人物。
- **SH02 CLOSE 6s（hook）** 雨桐俯视脚边发亮的手机，面部特写侧光。屏幕蓝光从下方打亮她的
  下颌与雨珠，霓虹红蓝作轮廓光。她皱眉、屏住呼吸。固定机位微推；与前一镜同雨势、同光向。
- **SH03 MEDIUM 5s（setup）** 雨桐蹲下拿起手机的中景，黑色风衣下摆沾雨发亮，胶片相机随动作
  摆动，屏幕亮起照亮半张脸。与前一镜同轴肩后角度；从伸手到握起一个完整动作。
- **SH04 CLOSE 5s（turn）** 手机屏幕特写过渡到雨桐瞳孔反光：屏幕上是她自己在这个天台被偷拍
  的照片。她抬眼环顾。冷蓝画面中屏幕暖光是唯一暖色；一次干净的焦点转移（屏幕→眼睛）。
- **SH05 CLOSE 6s（escalation）** 快速翻动相册的手指特写与她逐渐失色的表情交替：每张照片都
  是她，不同夜晚，同一天台。雨声渐密，霓虹闪烁频率加快，手持感轻微增强但不破坏构图。
- **SH06 CLOSE 5s（cliffhanger action）** 雨桐猛然停住——手机开始震动响铃。竖屏下半是亮起的
  来电界面，上半是她僵住的脸。同一光位、同一雨势。
- **SH07 CLOSE 6s（cliffhanger reveal）** 来电界面极近特写：来电人名字是「雨桐」。铃声持续，
  在她按下接听前一帧收黑；保留雨声与铃声，硬切黑场。

What today's pipeline emits for the same brief (for contrast): scene heading
`EXT. 城市天台上发现了一部不属于她的手机 - NIGHT`, actions like
`雨桐进入城市天台上发现了一部不属于她的手机`, canned dialogue `你终于来了` — grammatically
broken, premise-contradicting, no camera/lighting/continuity vocabulary.
