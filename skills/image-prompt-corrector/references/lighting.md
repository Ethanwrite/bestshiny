# Lighting decisions

Describe light as a controllable system:

- motivation and source;
- direction and height;
- apparent size, distance, hardness, falloff, intensity, and color temperature;
- key-to-fill relationship and negative fill;
- shadow density, background separation, rim control, exposure priority, and specular control.

Prefer concrete descriptions such as “large diffused key from camera-left, slightly above eye level,
soft opposing fill, subtle negative fill for contour, controlled rim separation.” Keep established light
unchanged for a targeted reference edit unless the user requests relighting.

For products, shape reflections rather than merely increasing brightness. For portraits, retain skin
microtexture and avoid clipping forehead, cheekbone, nose, and lip highlights.
